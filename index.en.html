What Did the AI Score Actually Measure?

Everyone says they're number one, but which test's number one is it?

Starting from how to score a toxic-comment classifier (80:20, stratify, confusion matrix), this climbs one step at a time to the exam papers and scoring methods for large language models (LLMs). Every experiment can be run directly in your browser, and at the end the Fruit Fly Researcher and AbaBot face off in a "leaderboard score-raising" showdown.
